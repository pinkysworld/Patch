\long\def\PatchReplaceConclusion#1\section*{Data and code availability}{%
Patch explores an architecture in which modeled post-creation application-state mutation is factored through explicit semantic Changes rather than ordinary reassignment. The restriction is by construction; the paper's contribution lies in what can be connected to and derived from it.

For RQ1, the Lean bridge directly connects a well-formed supported committed Change with a directional contract Effect and proves that an allowed bounded rule constrains the actual before/after magnitude. Bounded unknown magnitude is fail-closed, and the executable checker is equivalent to the relational rule/signature policy semantics for the modeled fragment.

For RQ2, the artifact additionally checks normalized signature/policy, integer ranges, finite exact/interprocedural calls, guarded/transitive traces, and selected direct WebAssembly runtime evidence while keeping source analysis, validators, capture, lowering, and engine correctness outside the theorem. The interprocedural and runtime layers specifically test whether the semantic effect/signature relationship survives helper propagation and repeated dynamic invocation. For RQ3, the explicitly defined target-only ablation isolates four policy distinctions that disappear when operation/magnitude are erased, while both variants retain target-escape detection.

The exploratory public real-code audit adds a different kind of evidence. Across 18 purposively selected retained-state mutation observations from six pinned public projects, five are direct local current-surface fits, eleven require adapters, and two require restructuring; three local observations match the narrow numeric directional theorem shape. Eleven of the 18 observations also expose non-standalone context constraints such as coupled targets, host persistence, sequential rebuilds, or dynamic targeting. These mixed results support external relevance of the semantic mutation vocabulary while identifying concrete current language boundaries; they do not establish prevalence, migration cost, security benefit, or superiority.

The strongest remaining empirical tests are therefore a larger independently coded or preregistered corpus, behavior-preserving translation studies, controlled fixed-machine assurance-cost measurement, and a fair implemented/formal comparison with a representative modern effect/capability approach. The present paper does not assume their outcomes.
